ABOUT US

Meaningful work. Not bullshit.

We started Unicorncat because most technology consulting is theater — decks, frameworks, and invoices with nothing running at the end. We do the opposite.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Technology should make work more human, not less. The point of automating the busywork is to give people back the time to do the work that actually matters.

We help small and mid-sized companies adopt AI the honest way: find the two or three places it genuinely helps, roll it out so it sticks, and train your team to keep going without us. When a tool doesn't exist, we build it. When AI is the wrong answer, we say so.

No account managers. No junior team learning on your budget. When you hire Unicorncat, you work directly with the two people who founded it.
